## 1.0.1

First release.

- SMS and MMS (photos, groups, subjects), delivery reports and dual SIM that never switches SIM silently.
- Undo send (5 seconds) and "Undo" after deleting; 30-day trash.
- Scheduled messages (day in a dropdown, "Tomorrow 9 am" shortcut), drafts and quick replies.
- Possible spam folder, decided on the phone, and optional cleanup of verification codes.
- Private conversations with fingerprint or PIN, and optional app lock.
- Starred messages, message details, search inside a conversation and "go to date".
- iPhone and Google Messages reactions shown as an emoji on the original message.
- Backup in the "SMS Backup & Restore" format.
- Pin, archive, mute, per-conversation sound, blocking, swipe actions (dropdowns in Settings) and multi-select.
- Liquid, Material (yellow), Samsung and iPhone icons.
